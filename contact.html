Frequently Asked Questions

Is the demo personalized to our specific hardware?

Yes. We do not show generic slideshows. We configure our Decision simulation engine to match the parameters, tolerances, and sensor variables of your exact industrial assets (e.g. SCADA systems, modular telemetry feeds) to demonstrate live recommendation outputs.

Do we need to prepare any data before the call?

Preparation is optional. Providing us with sanitized telemetry logs or summaries of historical failure incidents allows our engineers to preload your scenario before the demo, saving valuable control room evaluation time.

How long does it take to deploy Decision?

Because Decision is strictly a read-only integrator layer requiring zero write or execution authority, initial telemetry routing and dashboard synchronization takes less than 72 hours under standard enterprise security loops.
