Privacy Policy

How Decisio handles information across our website, product demonstrations, and decision-intelligence services.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

01

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Decisio Inc. ("Decisio," "we," "us," or "our") may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, request a demonstration, communicate with our team, or use Decisio services that reference this Policy.

Decisio is designed as a read-only decision-intelligence layer for industrial operations. Information handled through a customer environment may be subject to additional contractual terms, security requirements, data-processing agreements, and customer instructions.

02

Information We Collect

Contact & Account Information

Name, work email, company, role, industry, and other details you provide when requesting a demo, communicating with us, or creating an account.

Scenario Information

Operational summaries, incident descriptions, plant context, configuration details, or other material you choose to provide for a personalized demonstration.

Technical Information

IP address, browser and device information, approximate location, pages viewed, referral information, and similar website telemetry.

Communications

Messages, support requests, meeting details, questionnaire responses, and correspondence with Decisio personnel.

We ask customers and prospects not to submit passwords, private keys, credentials, classified information, or unnecessary personal information in demo forms or free-text operational descriptions.

03

How We Use Information

  • Provide, configure, demonstrate, maintain, and improve Decisio services.
  • Respond to requests, schedule demonstrations, and communicate with customers and prospects.
  • Understand product usage, diagnose issues, and improve reliability and user experience.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, protect systems, and investigate incidents.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, contractual, and operational obligations.

Where customer operational data is used within a Decisio environment, access and processing should be limited to the purposes agreed with the customer and the configuration of that environment.

04

Sharing & Disclosure

Decisio does not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the website or services, professional advisers, enterprise vendors, or authorities where required by law.

Service providers are expected to handle information only for authorized purposes and under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We may also disclose information as part of a corporate transaction, provided applicable confidentiality and legal requirements are addressed.

05

Retention & Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, resolve disputes, maintain business records, or satisfy legal obligations.

Decisio uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information involved. Access controls, encryption, isolation practices, logging, and least-privilege principles may be used depending on the service and customer configuration.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we design our services to reduce unnecessary access and exposure.

06

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold about you. You may also have the right to withdraw certain consents.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You can also control certain cookies through your browser or our cookie controls where available.

07

International Use

Decisio may operate with infrastructure, personnel, or service providers in multiple countries. Where information is transferred across borders, we will use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law and the relevant customer or contractual arrangement.

08
