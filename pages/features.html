Precision Capability Matrix

Every Feature Supports Better Decisions

Decisio's feature set is purpose-built for industrial decision intelligence — from the first diagnostic question to long-term institutional memory. No bloat, no dashboards for dashboards' sake. Every capability exists to help your team make better calls, faster.

01

Guided Diagnostic Questioning

Decisio asks targeted, context-aware questions to rapidly gather the information operators have but systems don't. No forms, no checklists — an intelligent conversation that adapts in real time.

02

Process Failure Detection

Goes beyond mechanical failure to identify breakdowns in process, communication, and handover. Recognizes that most incidents are systemic, not just technical.

03

Decision Brief Generation

Automatically generates structured decision briefs with root cause analysis, ranked options, risk assessment, and safety constraints — ready for the decision-maker in minutes, not hours.

04

Safety as Constraints

Safety rules aren't suggestions — they're hard constraints woven into every recommendation. Lockout/tagout, regulatory requirements, and site-specific protocols are enforced by default.

05

Intelligent Escalation

Knows when a situation exceeds the current operator's authority or expertise. Automatically suggests escalation paths with full context attached, so the next person up doesn't start from zero.

06

Expert Knowledge Capture

Captures the reasoning and judgment of experienced operators during real decisions. Transforms tribal knowledge into searchable, reusable institutional intelligence.

07

Institutional Decision Memory

Every decision — what was considered, what was chosen, and what happened — is logged and indexed. Future incidents reference past decisions, building an ever-improving knowledge base.

08

Read-only Integrations

Connects to SCADA, CMMS, historians, and sensor networks in read-only mode. Decisio ingests data but never writes back, never executes, and never controls. Trust by design.
