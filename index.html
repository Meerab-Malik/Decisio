OPERATING UNDER HAZARD

Why decisions fail in industrial operations

01

Failures aren't only technical

Most industrial incidents trace back to decisions made under extreme cognitive load. Teams have high-fidelity sensors but lack high-fidelity judgment tools.

02

Decisions happen under pressure

When an alarm flashes, operators have seconds. They face fragmented dashboard readouts scattered across legacy telemetry architectures, creating analysis paralysis.

03

Wrong decisions cost more than failures

An incorrect emergency action can trigger systemic failures, safety incidents, and cascading electrical violations — often resulting in millions in unneeded losses.
