01

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files or similar technologies stored on a device when you visit a website. They can help remember preferences, understand how visitors use a site, maintain security, and support website functionality.

This Policy applies to cookies and similar technologies used on the public Decisio website and related web properties that link to this Policy.

02

Cookie Categories

CategoryPurposeTypical Use
Strictly NecessaryCore website operation and security.Navigation, form handling, session or security functions.
PreferencesRemember choices and presentation settings.Language, interface preferences, or similar convenience settings.
AnalyticsUnderstand traffic and website performance.Page views, navigation patterns, and aggregated usage measurements.

Decisio may not use every category on every page. The exact technologies used can change as the website and measurement stack evolve.

03

Your Cookie Controls

You can usually manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

Browser Controls

Most browsers let you block, delete, or limit cookies through privacy and security settings.

Site Preferences

Where a consent or cookie-control interface is provided, use it to manage optional technologies.

04

Third-Party Technologies

Decisio may use third-party providers for analytics, scheduling, communications, security, or embedded services. Those providers may place their own cookies or similar technologies subject to their own privacy practices.

We seek to use third-party technologies that are appropriate for the purpose, limited to the data needed, and configured consistently with applicable requirements.

05

Cookie Lifetimes

Some cookies last only for the duration of a browser session, while others may remain for a defined period to remember settings or measure returning visits. Actual lifetimes depend on the technology and configuration in use at the time.

06

Relationship to Our Privacy Policy

Information collected through cookies may be personal information under applicable law. Our Privacy Policy explains how Decisio handles personal information more broadly.
